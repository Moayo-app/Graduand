**The structure of this Service narrows the processing.** The Service creates no accounts, the diagnosis involves no sign-in and no tokens, and the judgment runs in the customer's CI environment. The Company's infrastructure does not read the customer's repositories, and **repository names and judgment contents are not transmitted to the Company's servers in any mode.** Every section below stands on that.

## 1. Purposes of processing

The Company processes personal data for the following purposes and uses it for no others. If a purpose changes, we give notice in advance and obtain any consent required.

1. **Performance of the paid subscription contract** — confirming activation of the subscription and delivering the subscription benefit
2. **Responding to inquiries**
3. **Compliance with legal obligations**

The aggregate carries no value that identifies a user, and repository names and judgment contents are not transmitted to the Company's servers in any mode.

## 7. Destruction of personal data — procedure and method

When a retention period ends or the purpose of processing is achieved, the data is destroyed without delay. Destruction is carried out by means that make recovery impossible. Records preserved under a legal obligation are destroyed in the same way after the preservation period ends.

## 8. Measures to secure safety

- **Minimal collection by structure** — accounts, passwords and repository access tokens are not accepted in the first place. Not creating a place for leaks is the first measure.
- **Protection in transit** — communications use encrypted channels (TLS).
- **Minimal handlers** — the number of people handling personal data is kept to a minimum (currently one).
- **Access control** — the only accounts that can reach the settings of the host where the Service runs, and the records it keeps, belong to the handlers named above.
- **Access records** — the Company does **not keep access records itself.** The host where the Service runs does keep them, and the Company **can access** them — those records contain the address requested and the **IP address**. The Company looks at them for two purposes only: **to check for faults and abuse**, and **to read the two counts in Section 2**. They are not used to trace an individual user or to build per-user records, and the Company does not copy them to its own side. Because the host is the party writing the records, the items they contain and how long they are kept are determined by the host.

**If the Company becomes aware of a leak of personal data**, it will notify the affected users and report to the competent authorities without delay, and immediately take measures to reduce the harm.

## 9. Rights of data subjects and legal representatives, and how to exercise them — the process for access and correction

You may at any time demand **access** (including requests for disclosure), **correction, deletion, and suspension of processing** of your personal data.

**How to exercise them**: make the demand to the contact in Section 11; you may also act through a legal representative or an authorized person. The Company takes action and informs you of the result within 10 days of receiving the demand. If you disagree with the outcome, you may raise an objection through the same channel, and the Company will inform you of the result of its review and the reasons within 60 days of receiving the objection. Separately from the Company, you may also apply for dispute mediation or counsel to the competent bodies, such as the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee.

## 10. Automatic collection devices and how to refuse them · Do Not Track signals · third-party cross-site collection

- The Company places no cookies or identifiers that identify you or follow you over time. The aggregate in Section 2 is the **per-path request counts kept by the host**; it counts only occurrences of events and uses no identifiers.
- **Do Not Track signals** — as there is no tracking that identifies a user and follows them across sites and services, the Company does not respond separately to Do Not Track signals. The fact that it does not respond is itself stated in this section.
- **Third-party cross-site collection** — the Company does **not allow** third parties to collect users' behavioral information across sites and services through this Service. No advertising tools and no advertising identifiers are used.
